# Shared Product Specifications Reference

[type: shared]

## Outerwear — General Standards

- Hero shot must show full silhouette including hem
- Collar/lapel must be shown in its natural resting position (not styled up unless instructed)
- Sleeve length relative to the wrist is a key quality signal — show it
- If double-breasted: shoot buttoned. If single-breasted: both buttoned and open are needed.
- Lining shots: include one detail image of lining quality if premium material

## Knitwear — General Standards

- Texture is the story — macro detail shot required for all knitwear
- Show how the fabric drapes at hem and cuff — this communicates weight and quality
- Pilling disclaimer: include in PDP copy for any merino or cashmere blend
- Size note: include fit guidance (slim/true/relaxed) in every PDP

## Tailoring — General Standards

- Shoulder seam position is critical — must be visible in at least one shot
- Trouser break: document intended break (full/half/no break) in shoot brief
- Button quality: close-up detail required for suits above €200 price point

## Denim — General Standards

- Show waistband and back pocket placement in at least one shot
- Leg opening width and ankle length are key decision signals — frame accordingly
- Fade pattern (if applicable): document intentional vs. natural fade

## Accessories — General Standards

- Scale reference required: show on or near a body part
- Hardware detail shot mandatory for bags and belts
- Proportion shots: show with an outfit context, not isolated
