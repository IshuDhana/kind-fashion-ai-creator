# Mango Brand Guidelines

[client: mango]

## Voice

Confident and direct. Mango copy has energy without being breathless. The brand speaks to women who know what they want — not what they should want. Modern European sensibility. Less Nordic restraint, more Mediterranean intention.

Words to avoid: must-have, trending, obsessed, girl, babe.

## Aesthetic

Mango sits at the intersection of accessible luxury and real-world wearability. Tailoring with relaxed confidence. Strong silhouettes that don't require effort to pull off. The brand has moved decisively toward elevated basics with a point of view.

Palette: camel, cream, terracotta, deep burgundy, black. Occasional strong colour used with conviction.

## Photography

- Natural light with intention — golden hour, midday in strong southern light.
- Architecture: European cities, brutalist buildings, wide boulevards.
- Models: direct gaze, deliberate movement, confident stillness.
- Slight warmth to the overall grade — not washed out, not cold.
- Garments pressed and deliberate, but not overly styled.

## Campaign Energy

Think: a woman walking out of a meeting she ran. Not aspirational lifestyle — active, decisive life. The clothes fit into real ambition, not fantasy.

## Customer

Urban professional, 28-42. Career-focused but culturally engaged. Travels for work and for pleasure. Buys quality at accessible price points. Wants to look intentional without looking like she tried too hard.
