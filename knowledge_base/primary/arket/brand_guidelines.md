# Arket Brand Guidelines

[client: arket]
[updated: 2025]

## Voice

Arket speaks with restraint. Short sentences. No exclamation marks. No enthusiasm for its own sake. The brand believes in things that last — in language and in product. Copy should feel like something a thoughtful person said, not something a brand wrote.

Words to avoid: effortless, timeless, versatile, perfect, luxurious, chic, must-have.

## Aesthetic

Scandinavian functionalism without the coldness. Natural materials in their truest form. The aesthetic lives in texture: raw concrete, pale limestone, undyed linen. Never pristine white cyc — Arket's world has surface and weight.

Palette: ecru, undyed natural, warm stone, slate. Occasional deep navy or forest green as anchor tones.

## Photography Style

- Natural, diffused light only. No artificial fill.
- Late afternoon or overcast northern daylight preferred.
- One garment per frame where possible. The product is the story.
- Backgrounds: architectural texture, natural materials. Never graphic or decorative.
- Models: relaxed posture, weight shifted. Not posed, not casual — considered.

## Customer

Values longevity over trend. Buys less but thinks carefully. Works in design, architecture, education. Travels deliberately. Cooks. Reads. Does not need to be told how to style things.

## Tone Spectrum

- Product: factual, material-focused, dry
- Campaign: reflective, unhurried, slightly philosophical
- Social: brief, observational, never promotional
