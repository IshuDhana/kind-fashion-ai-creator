## Example 1 — Wool Overcoat

A double-faced wool overcoat cut wide through the shoulder and tapered slightly at the hem. The weight gives it structure; the construction gives it longevity. The lining is silk for warmth without bulk. Wears well over tailoring or knitwear. Improves with use.

**Performance:** 4.2% conversion rate, low return rate (3.1%)

## Example 2 — Merino Rollneck

A fine-gauge merino rollneck with a relaxed body and slightly extended sleeve. The neck rolls twice. The fabric is from a Portuguese mill we've worked with since 2019. Machine washable at 30°. Some pilling over time — this is the nature of merino, not a defect.

**Performance:** 5.1% conversion, 2.8% return — highest performing knitwear PDP

## Example 3 — Straight-Leg Denim

Cut from Japanese selvedge denim with a mid-rise and a straight leg that sits just above the ankle. Washed once during production to reduce shrinkage. Will fade and shape to the wearer over time. Size up if between sizes.

**Performance:** 3.9% conversion

## Patterns from top performers:
- Open with how the garment behaves or ages, not what it looks like
- Include one specific material provenance detail
- Address a potential concern proactively (pilling, sizing, care)
- End with a wearing note, not a styling suggestion
- Under 110 words in all cases
