# Arket Shoot Direction Reference

[client: arket]

## Lighting Philosophy

Never create light. Find it. Arket shoots rely entirely on available natural light — diffused northern daylight, overcast skies, or late afternoon sun at low angle. Fill cards only when absolutely necessary and used minimally. The goal is shadow that describes the garment's construction, not light that flatters.

## Background Logic

Surfaces with history. Raw concrete, pale limestone, whitewashed brick with visible texture, untreated wood. Nothing decorative. Nothing that competes with the garment. The background should feel like a place someone might actually be, not a set.

Avoid: pure white cyc, seamless paper backdrops, anything that reads as "studio fashion".

## Pose Direction

- Weight on the back foot. Hip slightly shifted. Nothing symmetrical.
- Arms long and relaxed — no hand-on-hip, no crossed arms unless the garment requires it.
- Gaze: direct or looking past the camera. Never aspirational upward gaze.
- Collar, cuffs, hem: leave as the garment falls naturally. Do not "style" unless correcting a genuine issue.
- Movement shots: walking away, turning — no "caught mid-laugh" energy.

## Framing

Wide enough to show silhouette in context. Tight enough to communicate material quality. Avoid cropping at joints. One garment per hero shot. Layering shots should still have a clear lead piece.

## What Arket Is Not

Not Zara (trend-driven, high contrast, commercial energy). Not COS (architectural rigidity). Not &Other Stories (eclectic, prop-heavy). Arket is quieter than all of them.
