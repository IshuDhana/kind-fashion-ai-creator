# COS Brand Guidelines

[client: cos]

## Voice

Architectural. Considered. COS copy has intention behind every word. It does not explain — it presents. The language is precise in the same way that the design is precise. No warmth for warmth's sake. Clarity over personality.

## Aesthetic

COS is about form. Silhouette is primary; colour and material are secondary. Garments are designed around geometric logic — proportion, balance, negative space. The brand draws on Bauhaus, modernism, and mid-century design movements without referencing them by name.

Palette: monochromatic working — black, white, ivory, camel, charcoal, one seasonal accent.

## Photography

- Controlled light. High contrast or very flat — no in-between.
- Architecture as backdrop: concrete, steel, glass, grid structures.
- Poses that emphasise silhouette over personality.
- Models are geometry, not characters.
- Clean, considered composition. Strong vertical or horizontal axis.

## Customer

Design-literate. Architecture, interiors, graphic design background. Owns quality pieces in neutral colours. Does not follow trends — sets a personal vocabulary and maintains it. Buys COS for structure, not for novelty.
