# Fashion Market Trends — S/S & A/W 2025

[type: secondary_research]
[source: WGSN, Lyst Index, Business of Fashion]

## Macro Direction

The post-maximalism correction continues. Consumers fatigued by logmania and loud branding are returning to garments that communicate through quality and cut rather than branding. "Quiet luxury" has peaked as a trend term but its underlying value system is now baseline expectation for mid-to-premium brands.

## Key Signals

**Tailoring dominance continues:** Relaxed-shoulder tailoring is up 34% in search volume year-on-year. The boxy blazer cycle is mature — now moving toward more deliberate cuts with interesting construction details (unstructured chest, deconstructed lapel).

**Knitwear as outerwear:** Chunky knit coats and heavy-gauge cardigans worn as outerwear increased 28% in sell-through. Material weight and texture are the primary purchase drivers.

**Denim stabilisation:** Wide-leg denim peaked in 2024 and is now normalised. Straight leg is dominant. Ultra-wide and barrel cuts are still performing in younger demographics.

**E-commerce imagery shift:** Brands using AI-generated imagery report 12-18% improvement in page engagement when imagery is indistinguishable from photography. However, 67% of consumers say they can identify AI imagery — and trust drops significantly when detected.

## Competitor Activity

**Zara:** Aggressively using AI for product imagery. High volume, fast turnaround. Quality variable. Customer trust issue emerging.
**&Other Stories:** Still committed to photographer-led campaigns. Slower but authenticity premium maintained.
**Massimo Dutti:** Moving toward cleaner, less prop-heavy imagery. Investing in material quality communication.

## Customer Sentiment

Primary return driver across mid-premium fashion: fit uncertainty (41%) and material quality not matching expectations set by imagery (33%). Imagery that clearly communicates drape, weight, and construction reduces returns.
