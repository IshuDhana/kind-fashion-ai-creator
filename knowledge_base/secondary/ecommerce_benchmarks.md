# E-commerce Imagery Benchmarks — Fashion 2025

[type: secondary_research]

## Conversion Impact of Imagery

- **Number of images per PDP:** 4-6 images = optimal. Below 4 reduces conversion by 18%.
- **Video content:** Adding a short (8-15s) loop video increases conversion 23% on average.
- **Detail shots:** Close-up of material/construction increases conversion 11% vs. no detail shot.
- **Fit model diversity:** Showing 2 model sizes increases conversion 14% and reduces returns by 8%.

## Return Rate Drivers

Top reasons customers return fashion items (2024/25 data):
1. Fit different from expected (41%)
2. Material/quality not as shown (33%)
3. Colour different from screen (16%)
4. Changed mind (10%)

**Implication for imagery:** Every shot should communicate drape, weight, and construction. Material quality must be visually legible. Fit should be shown in motion or with natural body weight distribution (not pinned/styled).

## AI Imagery Performance

Brands using AI imagery report:
- 18% increase in production speed
- 12% improvement in page engagement (when AI imagery is high quality)
- 67% of consumers can identify AI imagery when quality is low
- Trust drops 31% when AI imagery is detected as inauthentic

**Key insight:** The quality bar for AI imagery is not "looks photographic" — it is "communicates the garment truthfully". Consumers forgive AI. They don't forgive misleading imagery.

## Platform-Specific Guidance

**Instagram:** Lifestyle and editorial perform better than pure product. Aspect ratio: 4:5 for feed.
**TikTok:** Movement, behind-the-scenes, and process content outperform polished editorial.
**Website PDP:** White or neutral background for primary shot. Lifestyle context for secondary shots.
